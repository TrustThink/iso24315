# Cybersecurity

METR systems depend on the trusted creation, management, distribution, and use of electronic traffic regulations across multiple systems, organizations, and jurisdictions. Cybersecurity controls protect the authenticity, integrity and availability of METR information throughout its lifecycle.

METR cybersecurity engineering work currently includes:

[METR Cybersecurity Use Cases](cybersecurity-use-cases.md)

Describes the cybersecurity activities needed to establish and maintain trust in METR operations, including trust management, identity and credential management, authorization, security monitoring, audit, and incident response.

[METR Threat, Vulnerability and Risk Assessment (TVRA)](metr-threat-vulnerability-risk-assessment.md)

Identifies threats, vulnerabilities, and associated risks to METR assets, systems, information exchanges, and interfaces. The TVRA provides a basis for deriving and validating cybersecurity requirements and for prioritizing appropriate security controls and architectural protections.
