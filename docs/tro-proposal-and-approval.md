# TRO Proposal and Approval

Bringing a TRO from idea to approved legal and electronic form is the heart of the rule-maker workflow.

## Typical non-emergent flow

1. **Propose** — A rule maker appointed by a jurisdictional entity (for example a road transport authority, national road administration, or city council for local streets) drafts a proposed TRO.
2. **Collect comments** — Bodies entitled to comment review the proposal. Examples include police, health and fire services, local transport authorities, and public transport operators. Permanent orders may also go to public consultation.
3. **Update** — The rule maker resolves accepted comments and prepares the legal document plus annexes (plans, maps, drawings, pictures).
4. **Approve and inform** — The TRO is approved; commenting bodies — especially enforcement agencies — are informed. Rejection returns the package for revision.
5. **Store legal TRO** — Sign and archive the human-readable legal TRO (wet signature/stamp or equivalent official process).
6. **Create electronic TRO** — Transform the approved order into a structured digital format.
7. **Sign electronic TRO** — Apply digital signature and certificate so authenticity can be verified downstream.

## Emergent and field scenarios

Not every TRO follows the full consultation path:

- **Field rule makers** — A contractor on road works or an authorized incident responder may be mandated to propose and approve an emergent TRO with little or no external comment.
- **Central support** — A traffic management centre may create or support emergent TROs for field personnel.
- **Activation without new drafting** — Authorized personnel may engage a dormant or inactive approved TRO (change engagement/activation) rather than writing a brand-new order.
- **Bypass path** — In specific emergent situations, proposal and approval may be combined under delegated power.

Delegation of who may propose and approve TROs is a matter of national organization and legislation.

## Outputs rule makers must produce

| Output | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Approved legal TRO | Authoritative human-readable record |
| Signed electronic TRO | Machine-processable equivalent for METR translation |
| Notifications | Enforcers and entitled bodies know what changed |

Approval alone does not put devices on the road or activate METR information. That is covered in [installation and implementation](tro-installation-and-implementation.md).
